### Property Specifications
Welcome to this charming single-family home located at 2848 Chromite Dr, Santa Clara, CA. Built in 1958, this beautifully remodeled home offers a cozy yet spacious living experience. With 1,383 square feet of living space, the home features 4 bedrooms and 2 bathrooms, all on a single level. Additionally, there is a 236 square foot bonus room, perfect for extra living space or a home office. The property sits on a 5,600 square foot lot and includes a 2-car attached garage, providing ample parking and storage.

### Renovation History
This home has been thoughtfully updated with a new electrical panel, HVAC system, and plumbing. The gourmet kitchen is a highlight, featuring custom wood cabinetry, quartz countertops, and stainless steel appliances. The bathrooms have been updated with new vanities, faucets, mirrors, toilets, and doors. Throughout the home, you'll find waterproof wood flooring, fresh interior and exterior paint, and new double-paned windows. The addition of 30 LED recessed lights brightens the space beautifully.
